MergeDoku 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年8月15日

MergeDoku 重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

我们存储什么

游戏进度（关卡、分数、物品、设置偏好），仅存储在您的设备本地。我们不会将这些数据上传到任何服务器。

我们不收集什么

我们不收集个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片等）。

我们不会向任何第三方出售您的个人数据。

第三方广告（Google AdMob）

本应用通过 Google AdMob 展示广告。AdMob 及其合作伙伴可能收集和处理以下类型的数据：

  • 设备广告标识符（如 Android 的 GAID / iOS 的 IDFA）
  • IP 地址
  • 设备与网络信息
  • 大致地理位置
  • 应用使用与交互数据
  • 崩溃与诊断数据

这些数据用于投放广告、频次控制、归因以及防止欺诈。Google 对这些数据的使用受 Google 隐私权政策 约束。

个性化广告与退出

AdMob 可能使用广告标识符向您展示个性化广告。您可以随时在设备设置中限制或退出个性化广告：

  • Android：设置 → Google → 广告 →「删除广告 ID」或「退出广告个性化」
  • iOS：设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 → 关闭本应用的跟踪权限

退出后您仍会看到广告，但广告与您的关联度会降低。

儿童隐私

本游戏面向全年龄段。对于适用地区法律认定为未成年人的用户，我们会配置 AdMob 仅展示非个性化广告，且不收集用于个性化广告的标识符。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

数据删除

删除本应用即可永久清除所有本地游戏数据。您也可以通过在设备设置中重置广告标识符来清除广告相关数据。

MergeDoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

MergeDoku values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

What we store

Game progress (levels, scores, items, preferences), stored locally on your device only. No data is uploaded to any server.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, location, contacts, photos, etc.).

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process the following types of data:

  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g., GAID on Android / IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address
  • Device and network information
  • Approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Crash and diagnostic data

This data is used for ad serving, frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Personalized Ads & Opt-Out

AdMob may use advertising identifiers to show you personalized ads. You can limit or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable tracking for this app

You will still see ads after opting out, but they will be less relevant to you.

Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages. For users legally considered minors in applicable regions, we configure AdMob to serve non-personalized ads only, and identifiers for personalized ads are not collected. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

Deleting this app permanently removes all local game data. You can also reset your advertising identifier in device settings to clear ad-related data.
